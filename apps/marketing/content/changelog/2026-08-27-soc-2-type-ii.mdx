Superset has completed its SOC 2 Type II audit. An independent auditor watched our security controls run for months, not just their design on a single day, and issued the final report this week. It builds on the [penetration test we passed earlier this year](/changelog/2026-03-30-mobile-agents-themes-editor).

Security has shaped Superset from the start. Your repos, terminal output, and agent sessions stay on your machine. Cloud sync carries account and organization metadata only, and the full source is on GitHub to audit. The Type II report adds independent, ongoing verification of the controls behind all that, from access reviews to change management and monitoring.

Request the report and review our security documentation at [trust.superset.sh](https://trust.superset.sh). If your security team is evaluating Superset, [talk to us](/enterprise).
