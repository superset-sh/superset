## Post 1

Your agents can now use your apps with an @mention in @superset_sh.

Pick a plugin in the prompt and tell your agent what you need. We also added connections for Superhuman, Stripe, Sentry, Granola, Circleback, and YNAB.

## Post 2

Also this week: live Linear issues in Tasks and one place to find and retry failed automation runs.

A peek at what's next 👀

Native iPad support, cloud automations, and agent chat are on the way. We're also prototyping voice on mobile. Ask what your agents are doing, then tell one what to do next.

Plus a bunch of bug fixes. Thanks to everyone in the v2 open beta for the reports, please keep them coming!
