Hover — the newest reply overlays the thumbnail, then leaves.

The count Every comment on the page, across threads and versions, in plain white beside the title. A small green dot badges the icon’s corner while unresolved threads remain; it disappears once the conversation settles. Screen readers get the whole phrase: “5 replies, 2 open threads.”
The peek Hovering or focusing the count — not the card — reveals the newest reply: avatar, author, first 240 characters. Per CommentPreviewCard.tsx, hover waits 180ms and keyboard focus opens instantly; Escape closes. Hover-card grace: the pointer can travel from the count into the peek — a 300ms window keeps it open — and clicking the peek opens the thread too. Clicking the count opens the page with that comment’s thread selected in the panel; clicking the rest of the card opens the page. Agent replies carry the bot mark and the Agent byline.
The default No comments, no chrome: a quiet page’s card is identical to today’s, and the settled-gray count is the loudest thing a finished conversation ever gets.

How it’s wired

One query
page.list gains two correlated laterals: an aggregate for commentCount and openThreadCount, and a limit-1 for the newest reply. The hover peek costs nothing — the data is already on the row.
Additive shape
The new fields ride RouterOutputs, so mobile, the CLI, and MCP pages_list inherit them without breakage.
Localized
The two new plural strings are hand-translated in all 17 locales, reusing each catalog’s established words for “reply” and “thread.”

Calls to make

Dot semantics The green dot currently means any unresolved thread. “New since you last opened it” is the sharper signal, but needs per-viewer read state we don’t track yet.
A needs-reply filter With open counts on every row, a filter beside All, Pinned, and Team is a small follow-up. Left out to keep this change tight.
